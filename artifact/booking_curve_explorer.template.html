How to read a curve

The right edge is the stay night. Moving left goes further out. "Net on the books" at 30 days is every room-night booked 30 or more days ahead, minus those already canceled by then.

"% of final stayed" divides that by the room-nights that ended as checked-out. It runs above 100% when more was on the books than eventually stayed; the gap is the wash still to come.

What is derived

Booking date is arrival date minus lead time. Cancellation date is the status date of canceled bookings. No-shows stay on the books until the night itself and are not counted as stayed.

ADR is one average per booking, applied evenly to each of its nights. It is not a nightly rate schedule.

What is missing

Date changes, rate changes and room moves are not recorded, so a booking appears with its final dates from the day it was made. Real pickup reports would differ.

Stay nights after 31 Aug 2017 are left out because the file only includes arrivals up to that date.
